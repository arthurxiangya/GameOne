Menu

  • Characters with a gold ! have a story for you; a ? means something to hand in. Elder Maren by the fountain starts the main story.
  • Open the journal (J) to see every story, choose which one to follow, and check today's bounties from Captain Rook.
  • Visit Smith Oda to buy tonics, sharpen your blade, buy a mount or a pet egg, and trade Ember Marks.
  • Press R to ride your mount (taking a hit or using a skill puts you back on foot) and P for your stable and pets. Eggs hatch after you win enough fights.
  • Tap a monster to target it, then use your skills. Strike repeats while held.
  • Other heroes you see are real players. Say hello in chat.
